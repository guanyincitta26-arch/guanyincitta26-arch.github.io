Your backup is a file you keep.

There is no cloud copy of your data, so you are always in charge of it. Save a backup whenever you like and keep it wherever you trust.

  1. Export a backupIn Settings, tap Export a backup. Budget makes one small file.
  2. Save it somewhere safeChoose where it goes: your files, a drive, or a message to yourself.
  3. Restore on any phoneOn a new phone, tap Import a backup and everything comes back.

Questions, answered.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no sign-in. Open the app, add your name and currency, and you are ready.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything you enter is stored on your phone. Budget has no server to send it to, and it does not use analytics or trackers.

What happens if I get a new phone?

Export a backup on your old phone, save the file somewhere you can reach, then import it on your new phone. Everything comes back as it was.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Budget works fully offline, all the time.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Budget is on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android.

How much does it cost?

The price is shown on the App Store and Google Play before you download. There are no ads, ever.
